Cats Vs Zombies

Use your Blender models

The map and player load automatically from models/MAP.glb and models/pusheen.glb. Choose a .glb file to replace the map or enemy.

Player character Loads from models/pusheen.glb

Start with the pistol and 10000 Dabloons. The 700-Dabloon box awards a random weapon. Press 1-6 to equip weapons you own; duplicate box rewards cost Dabloons but do not unlock a weapon. Guns and thrown weapons use magazines and reserve ammo; press R to reload. Mobs have a 15% chance to drop ammo for a random weapon except the Toaster; walk over the ammo box to pick it up. Weapon shots use weapons/bullet.glb; adjust damage with each weapon's damage value and firing speed with its fireRate value (shots per second) in WEAPON_DEFINITIONS near the top of game.js. The shotgun fires a spread, and the shuriken is thrown when you shoot. Creepers prioritize the bomb near spawn; it has 150 health and takes 25 damage per blast. In Level 8, the bomb shield shop rises from near the bomb while gameplay pauses; after it appears, press E nearby to buy a blue shield for 1000 Dabloons. It absorbs Creeper blasts and has 125 health. Use the turret shop on a different side of the map to buy up to four turrets near the bomb. The shop is four times its original size. Turrets fire at nearby Creepers: normal turrets deal 0.2 damage at 8 shots per second; upgraded turrets deal 0.3 damage at 12 shots per second. Walk up to a turret and press E to add a crown for 1000 Dabloons; each turret can be upgraded once. The turret shooting.mp3 sound loops while a Creeper is in range. Enemy clips named Walk and Attack (or similar) are selected automatically; the Creeper uses a procedural walk cycle until its animation is exported. Level 8 is the Wither boss fight; after a two-second delay, two Creepers spawn and target the bomb. One more spawns every two seconds, with no limit on how many can be alive, until the Wither is defeated. The Wither hovers, fires aimed projectiles that explode on impact, and summons up to four Wither Skeletons. Its hurt, idle, shoot, and death sounds load from wither sound affects/.

Each defeated mob drops mob drops/money.glb; pick it up to receive 125 Dabloons.

There is a 25-second break between rounds, with a countdown at the top during the final 5 seconds.

Buy the Magnet at its for-sale sign for 400 Dabloons to attract mob drops automatically within 16 units.

Tree model loads automatically from models/tree.glb.
